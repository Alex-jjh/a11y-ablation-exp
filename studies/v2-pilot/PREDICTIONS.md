# v2 pilot: predictions

> **Summary:** Predictions written before any pilot agent run, from how the two harnesses build the observation and how the agent acts, not from any result. Two directional predictions form family F1 (names removal lowers success on strict on-path tasks, each model; one-sided, Holm), and four equivalence predictions form F2 (landmark and heading removal lower success by less than 10 points, each model; 90% interval against the margin). Three are must-hold controls. The rest are exploratory: sizes per kind and env, the names add-back and half-dose, the label-function sham, the format control, the browser-use contrast, noise and the two models. The power of every registered test is stated beside it. Every prediction is conditional on its cell's manipulation checks (DESIGN.md §6).

How to read an entry. **Effect** = paired drop in success, in percentage points: mean over admitted tasks of (clean baseline rate minus cell rate); positive means the cell does worse (ANALYSIS.md §2). Unless stated, the cell is harness H1 (BrowserGym AXTree text), on-path tasks by the primary flag, each model separately. **Band** = the cell's own rerun band (ANALYSIS.md §3), descriptive. **Half-width** = the expected 95% interval half-width for the cell's task count, given as a range over the three reference variance settings of `precision.json` (DESIGN.md §4.4). **Power** = probability that the registered test is graded hit at the stated true mean drop, same three settings (`power.json`; t approximation to the bootstrap, admission simulated). Labels: **F1** (directional, Holm), **F2** (equivalence), **must-hold** (a control; failure means the pipeline or the cell is wrong, not a finding about agents), **exploratory** (estimate and interval, no test claim). Grounds are close-read keys (`../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_<key>.md`), our records (`../../docs/feasibility/`), or "ours".

What each harness gives the agent (the mechanism all predictions rest on):

- **H1.** Text only: the accessibility tree of the viewport, one line per node with a bid, role and accessible name; StaticText inside a named element is hidden, and appears when the element loses its name; heading level never printed; one action per step addressed by bid; previous observations kept until 26,000 estimated tokens ([2026-10-29-breakingweb.md](../../docs/feasibility/2026-10-29-breakingweb.md); `breakingweb/agent.py`). The page is unchanged, so the action is still possible by mouse; whether it is still addressable by bid is checked per reference action by M10 (a heading turned `div`, for example, can lose its own line and bid).
- **H2.** Element lines only for interactive or scrollable elements, attributes such as `aria-label`, `role`, `image_alt`; visible text always printed; no landmark, heading or `th` element printed; plus the unchanged viewport screenshot ([2026-10-30-browseruse-footprint.md](../../docs/feasibility/2026-10-30-browseruse-footprint.md) §1, §3).
- In both, the agent finds its target by matching the task text to what the observation says about each element. Failure at a step usually hinges on one or two elements ([Enomoto2026a](../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_Enomoto2026a.md)), so a kind matters when it is the only carrier of what distinguishes the target.

## 1. Registered tests

### F1: directional (one-sided, Holm over 2 tests at family-wise 0.05)

| ID | Cell | Prediction | Size | Power at 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 | Mechanism | Where the information survives | Grading | Conditional on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PC1 (Opus), PC2 (GPT) | names, strict on-path (24 tasks before admission; half-width 6.2 to 13.3) | Drop > 0 | 10 to 30 points | 0.26-0.51 / 0.51-0.83 / 0.75-0.95 / 0.97-1.0 at the first Holm step (one-sided 0.025) | On strict tasks an acted-on button or link becomes `''` (icon buttons: star, notifications, submit search) or shares its new name with another control ("Add to Cart" on every product). The agent must pick a bid from position and neighbouring text. Acted-on Gemini actions on the measured paths: 237 empty, 318 ambiguous of 3,195 ([2026-10-30-on-path-dose.md](../../docs/feasibility/2026-10-30-on-path-dose.md) §3.1) | Visible text (BrowserGym falls back to it: `link 'Open X'` to `link 'X'`), sibling StaticText, tree position | **Hit** if Holm rejects; **miss** if the upper 95% bound is below 10 (the bottom of the predicted range); otherwise **unresolved** | M1-M3, M2a, M2b |

A drop below 10 points is likely to be graded unresolved: at a true 10 the test rejects 26 to 51% of the time. The pilot is not powered to detect drops below 15 points in 24 tasks; the main study's power on the sealed pool is in DESIGN.md §5.

### F2: equivalence (90% interval against a 10-point margin, each test a separate claim)

| ID | Cell | Prediction | P(hit) at true 0 / 5 / 10; P(miss) at true 15 | Mechanism | Where the information survives | Grading | Conditional on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PC3 (Opus), PC4 (GPT) | landmarks, on-path (64 tasks, minus M10 failures) | Drop below 10 points (expected 0 to 5) | 0.79-0.995 / 0.33-0.64 / 0.05-0.08; 0.28-0.41 | The agent acts by bid on controls; landmark lines name regions it rarely needs to choose a control. The variant removes the landmark lines and re-indents their subtrees; every control line, name and bid stays | All controls, text, order; landmark labels on other elements | **Hit** if the upper 90% bound is below 10; **miss** if the lower 90% bound is above 10; otherwise **unresolved**. A miss is then read against `fmt_wrap` (E-F1) | M1-M3, M10; `fmt_wrap` delivered |
| PC5 (Opus), PC6 (GPT) | headings, on-path (62 tasks, minus M10 failures) | Drop below 10 points (expected 0 to 5) | as PC3 | `heading 'X'` becomes `StaticText 'X'`: the word "heading" goes, the text stays. Level was never printed, so no level information is lost. Tasks where a reference action loses its bid (M10) are outside this test, so the prediction is about heading information, not action handles | Heading text as StaticText, its position | As PC3 | M1-M3, M10; machinery sham observation identical (MH5) |

The margin is 10 because the pilot can reach it: at margin 5, a true null is graded hit only 35 to 81% of the time (`power.json`). No multiplicity adjustment: each test is a separate claim about one kind and one model, and the joint claim "all four" is an intersection-union test, which needs none (ours; no close-read covers it).

Why these six: names is the only kind with a large on-path dose and a mechanism that leaves the agent without a distinguishing carrier; landmarks and headings are the kinds whose information the H1 agent receives but, by the mechanism, does not need for choosing actions. Labels strict (29 tasks) is not registered: its mechanism depends on placeholders and visible label text whose use is unmeasured, and the main study can test it on only 23 sealed tasks.

## 2. Must-hold controls

| ID | Cell | Prediction and rule | Sensitivity | Failure means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MH1 | `none` (own checkout, process and port), H1 and H2, each model | The two-sided test of drop = 0 at 0.05 does not reject | Power 0.23-0.55 at a 5-point pipeline effect, 0.63-0.97 at 10 (64 tasks) | Serving, process or schedule moves success; stop and find the cause before reading any kind |
| MH2 | do-nothing, every task and build | 0 passes | | The evaluator credits empty runs; the task leaves that cell (M9) |
| MH3 | `pos_info` (24 tasks, 2 runs), each model | The one-sided test of drop > 0 at 0.05 rejects (expected drop at least 30) | Detects a 30-point drop almost surely; says nothing about sensitivity at 10-15 points, which comes from the power above and from `names50` | The analysis or the sample cannot see a large information loss; every null in the pilot is unreadable |
| MH5 | Machinery shams (headings, landmarks, tableheads), H1 | Observation byte-identical to clean on every replay state (checked before runs, no agent) | | The sham is not a sham; fix before Stage 1 |

Grounds: positive and negative controls through the same pipeline ([Gorgen2018](../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_Gorgen2018.md)); a null agent scores zero ([Zhu2025](../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_Zhu2025.md), [Chen2026a](../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_Chen2026a.md)); the replay record's do-nothing result (0 of 195) ([2026-10-30-replay-feasibility.md](../../docs/feasibility/2026-10-30-replay-feasibility.md) §4). The size for MH3 is ours. `pc_break` is exploratory (E-P1): a small drop there has two readings, so it cannot be a must-hold.

## 7. Grading

- F1 and F2 predictions are graded **hit**, **miss** or **unresolved** by the rules in their tables. There is no "partly" grade (DQ-predict Gaps; ours).
- Exploratory predictions are reported with the estimate, the interval and the cell's band; an estimate inside the band is described as "inside the rerun band" ([Wei2026](../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_Wei2026.md), §9).
- A prediction whose manipulation check failed is reported as **not tested**.
- These files are frozen and timestamped at Stage 0r, before any study-model call. Every later change is a numbered deviation with its date and reason ([Chen2026a](../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_Chen2026a.md), [Niksirat2023](../../../ai-accessibility-paper/literature/papers/L_Niksirat2023.md)).
