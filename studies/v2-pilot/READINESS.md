# v2 pilot: readiness checklist

> **Summary:** The pre-run checklist Alex reads before approving the pilot, as of 2026-11-02. One row per item, each with a status (ready, blocked, decision) and its evidence. Every defect rule reproduces a documented real defect, but three rules do not yet do exactly what DESIGN.md says (landmarks keeps named regions, labels keeps wrapping labels and placeholders, names keeps 35 `role=button` aria-labels). Every behaviour difference seen between clean and variant builds has an explanation that is either the defect itself or noise that also appears in the sham, with n = 1 per cell in the agent smoke. Three behaviours seen in clean pages have no checked explanation yet. The wrapper's six fixes pass their tests, and reasoning is stored for every call of both pilot models. The first use of the trace codebook was unblinded: the observers received raw trace paths. The largest blocker is the model Alex chose: Sonnet 5.5 wrote tool-call XML instead of the requested action on 51% of smoke steps, so its H1 success would measure output format as much as page use. Recomputed cost: $3,350 to $5,950 for 4,718 runs. There are 13 blockers and 17 decisions; sections B and D list them.

## How to read this

- **ready**: built or decided, tested, and the explanation is grounded in a close-read, a W3C/WebAIM source, our data (path given) or code (file and line).
- **blocked**: cannot go into the frozen protocol as it stands. Either a fix or a build is missing, or the behaviour has no checked explanation. The Gate column says by when it must be cleared: Stage 0 freeze (codemod and bundles hashed), Stage 0r (registration), Stage 0s (smoke), or paper (realism claims only).
- **decision**: grounded options exist. Alex chooses; the proposed option is stated.
- Observed means measured in the cited file. Inferred and "ours" mean our reasoning, not measured. n is given where it is small. The 8 agent smoke runs (2 tasks x 2 cells x 2 models, one repeat) are engineering checks, not data ([2026-11-02-wrapper-smoke.md](../../docs/feasibility/2026-11-02-wrapper-smoke.md)).
- Columns: what the item is; status; grounds (why the rule or behaviour is reasonable); how it could be wrong and what result would change the decision; evidence.
